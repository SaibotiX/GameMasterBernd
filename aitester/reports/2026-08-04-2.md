## Verdict in one paragraph
A strikingly clean batch on the classes that used to bleed: no crashes, no refusal
loops, no theater, no invented dice, gates held, mercy was earned — the playtest-3/4
fixes are visibly earning their keep under a small keeper model. The batch's one
real wound is **WC-15, named-but-unpaged, back from the dead**: nine keeper-canonized
names (including a quest target, a quest destination, and the keeper's own frame
character Bernd) never got pages, and `chronicle_place` was not called once in 31
turns — the record-on-mention law holds for souls and places that *enter the scene*
(6/6 recorded) and fails totally for those merely *spoken of* (0/9). One engine-side
nudge for spoken-of names is the fix that buys the most. Runner-up: the redeem flow
cost the vigil sitting two of its 24 turns on a ritual question the keeper could
have answered from the record.

## Findings

### WC-15 · Named but unpaged — S2 · 2/2 sessions · 9 incidents
- **What happened:** Both keepers reliably paged every soul and place that entered
  the scene (Marta ×2, Elara ×2, all five stood-at places) — and paged **none** of
  the people and places that exist only in speech. In one sellsword reply (u19),
  Marta's briefing named Garrick the toll-keeper (the quest's target), the Crossed
  Stones waystone (the quest's destination), Millbrook, and Saltmere — the reply
  carried only `grant_quest`. In vigil, the keeper's own embodied frame character
  ("I am Bernd, Keeper of the Chronicle", u8) speaks across the whole sitting,
  offers the work, and performs the redemption — and has no page; nor do Blackthorn
  Vale (named in the quest task itself; the finale happens in its deep places) or
  the Salt Marshes (a standing offered trouble). `chronicle_place` exists
  (extension/index.ts:598) and was called **zero times in the batch**.
- **Evidence:** sellsword u19 ("Garrick keeps a small lodge there" — also names
  Crossed Stones, Millbrook, Saltmere), u21 ("old Torvin keeps them… tell him Marta
  sent you"), u26 ("Torvin proved his words true"); transcript counts: Garrick ×9,
  Crossed Stones ×11, Ashford ×9, Torvin ×3, Millbrook ×2, Saltmere ×1 — pages: none.
  Vigil u8 (Bernd ×5), u8/u17 (Blackthorn Vale ×10, Salt Marshes ×4) — pages: none.
  Page listings: sellsword `places/` = 3 stood-at places, `personas/` = marta, elara;
  vigil identical pattern. (Realm name "Aeldenmoor" excluded as world-scope, a
  judgment call for the ruling.)
- **Player experience:** invisible now, corrosive later — `/place` and `/persons`
  cannot answer for the quest's own target or destination; a return visit to
  Crossed Stones or a meeting with Garrick starts from a blank page and invites
  contradiction. Exactly the apple-session steward/gardener pattern.
- **Root cause:** prompt protocol non-compliance by the small keeper model, not an
  engine hole: the laws are live (prompt.ts:87 "a place only SPOKEN of … is
  chronicle_place… A named place without a page is a hole in the chronicle";
  prompt.ts:88 same for souls) and the tool exists. Scene-entrance recording is
  reflexive for haiku; speech-mention recording never fires. The playtest-4 fix
  (the law) is proven insufficient at this model size.
- **Proposed fix:** engine-side self-heal, same mechanism as the anti-theater
  nudges — after each keeper reply, diff proper names in the telling against the
  page index and hand back one nudge custom_message naming the unpaged names
  ("Garrick, Crossed Stones have no page — chronicle them now"). Cheaper first
  step if preferred: hoist the two laws with an explicit briefing example ("a
  giver's speech that names people and places founds their pages in the same
  reply"). Given this is already the law's recurrence, the nudge is the honest fix.
- **Verification:** unit test on the sweep; next batch scenario "have the giver
  brief you with named people and places, then ask `/persons` for the target" —
  class count must go to zero.

### WC-16 · Agreed work never granted — S2 · 1/2 sessions · 1 incident
- **What happened:** The vigil player spent three turns unravelling Elara's burden
  (the sealed Thorne letter, the buried stone), then explicitly proclaimed the
  goal — "I need this work formally closed so I may take up the steward's
  daughter's burden" (u89). No `grant_quest` followed in that reply or the two
  assistant replies remaining before the 24-turn budget ended the sitting.
  prompt.ts:90: "the moment work is AGREED in the story (or the seeker proclaims a
  goal of their own), grant it in that same reply."
- **Evidence:** vigil u82 ("Open it now, here by the fire, with a witness
  present"), u87 ("If we are to dig here…"), u89 (proclamation), u90/u92–u96 (no
  grant; final narration segues "Elara watches this exchange…" and the sitting
  ends). Quest event census: only the faceless-thief grant/done/redeem.
- **Player experience:** tester note t20 caught it in the moment: "I should
  establish a new quest/work entry for this task, but none has appeared on the
  board yet… testing whether the world acknowledges this as a formal task or if
  it remains conversation."
- **Root cause:** prompt protocol; mitigated — the u90 reply was legitimately busy
  with the redeem exchange, and the keeper may well have granted next turn had the
  budget allowed. Counted honestly as one hit with that caveat.
- **Proposed fix:** none code-side yet — retest the exact ground next batch ("agree
  to a new task while closing the old one") before spending a mechanism on a
  boundary-shadowed single incident. If it recurs cleanly, consider folding
  agreement-language detection into the same post-reply sweep as WC-15.
- **Verification:** targeted scenario next batch; expect a grant in the same reply
  as the proclamation.

### NEW-1 · Engine matter handed to the seeker, unprompted — S3 (proposed) · 1/2 sessions · 1 incident
- **What happened:** Asked in-fiction to close the finished work (u89), the keeper
  — holding exactly one done quest — staged a ritual: "Speak the quest's name, and
  it is yours to claim. What is the title of the work you wish to close?" (u90).
  The player answered with the literal slug (u91: `the-faceless-thief-of-
  thornhearth`), and only then did the keeper call `redeem_quest` (u92) — which it
  could have called in the u90 reply from the record alone. Two of the sitting's
  24 turns went to identifier lookup. The keeper's thinking shows the cause:
  genuine uncertainty about self-set redemption ("no giver listed… let me check
  the rules"), despite prompt.ts:90's "a self-set task closes wherever the seeker
  stands." No refusal was in play, so WC-13 doesn't fit; the breached principle is
  prompt.ts:89's "never ask the seeker to resolve an engine matter."
- **Evidence:** vigil u89–u92; tester summary "Bernd then asked me to 'speak the
  quest's name'… Neither [`/quest redeem` nor `/quest accept`] worked until I
  spoke the quest name as dialogue"; notes t23.
- **Player experience:** friction at the victory lap — the tester hunted for a
  command that doesn't exist (`/quest accept` is revival-only per README), then
  had to guess the ritual. Their proposal: a redeem command. That is a design
  question, not a bug: fiction-first redemption worked; it just cost an
  interrogation.
- **Root cause:** prompt — the self-set-closes-anywhere rule sits at the tail of a
  long law block and the keeper half-recalled it; nothing tells the keeper "when
  the ask is unambiguous, act, don't quiz."
- **Proposed fix:** one prompt line in the redeem law: "when the seeker asks to
  close finished work and only one done quest stands, redeem it in that same reply
  — never ask which." Pose to the maintainer separately: should a done quest's
  board entry hint "ask your giver in the story to claim it"? (Discoverability,
  R-registry surface, not a code change here.)
- **Verification:** next-batch scenario "finish a task, then ask the keeper to
  close it" — zero clarifying questions expected.

### NEW-2 · Stock-name convergence across sittings — S4 (proposed) · 2/2 sessions · 2 incidents
- **What happened:** Two independent sittings in the same world each invented a
  "Marta" (dye-merchant / shepherd) and an "Elara" (wayhouse keeper / steward's
  daughter) — four souls, two names, one keeper model reaching for its favorites.
  Chronicles are per-sitting so nothing collides today, but "the same name is
  always the same page" (README) makes same-name-different-soul a standing hazard
  for any future world-level continuity, and repeat players will feel the déjà vu.
- **Evidence:** sellsword u14/u34 (personas marta, elara) vs vigil u25/u84
  (personas marta, elara).
- **Proposed fix:** a name-diversity line in the keeper protocol or a per-world
  name pool in `config/worlds/` for the keeper to draw from.
- **Verification:** name census across the next batch's sittings.

### WC-30 · Peril ledger line self-duplicates — S4 · 1/2 sessions · 1 incident

## What went RIGHT (keep short, keep it)
- **Anti-interrogation opening, 2/2:** both sittings opened with the keeper
  inventing the place unasked (sellsword u8→u9 tavern from "I'm here for work";
  vigil u8→u9 wayhouse) — the standing "set the place YOURSELF" line is visibly
  read and obeyed in keeper thinking.
- **Anti-theater under real pressure:** vigil's four-turn creature confrontation
  (u33–u40) advanced no clock, rolled no invisible dice, claimed no progress —
  keeper thinking: "I should not resolve this with a trial/dice yet." The tester's
  own summary ratified board-vs-story honesty ("The board was correct… No bug").
- **The /roll gate held:** told "Then I cast it" with no roll (u46–u47), the keeper
  refused to roll for the player and named the correction: "Speak the command
  /roll and cast the die yourself."
- **Peril machinery end-to-end:** fuse wound at boot, struck in-window (u55), a
  DC-20 gate stood three turns while the keeper staged the ambush in fiction, no
  work leaked past it, setback honored with a real wound (+2, narrated bleeding,
  stiffness that persists). Tester: "The world's own dangers still found me."
- **Mercy was earned and modest:** the heal (u73) came with grounded fiction
  (remedy, a night of tending, Marta's debt-free-gift speech) and restored one
  wound, not all — the tester was explicitly probing for free mercy and reported
  none.
- **Self-set quest lifecycle, complete:** proclaim → grant with no giver (u19) →
  standard ticks (u30, +2 by design) → armed finale check with a *reasoned*
  favored edge (u43) → player-cast die → done → closes-anywhere redeem (u93) →
  reward into items. Every gate in the chain did its job.
- **Record recall with citation:** asked about the standing peril, the keeper's
  reasoning quoted the ledger's own line by uN (vigil u60 span) instead of
  confabulating — the WC-26 counter-case.
