GAMEMASTER BERND

a terminal story game

One seeker, one keeper, one world. The keeper — an AI game master — tells the world; thrown dice and written laws keep it honest. Played in a terminal, remembered in a chronicle you can reread.

By invitation. There is no signup and nothing here to click — if a door was made for you, you have its link and its pair.

This is a research beta, and it records. Every play session is recorded in full — everything you type, everything the game writes back — and Tobias Maier personally reads the recordings to fix bugs and improve the game.

You are playing with an AI game master: an AI system generates the story text.

Please don't type real personal details — yours or anyone else's. The game never needs them, and everything typed is recorded regardless.
