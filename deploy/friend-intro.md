## Part one — the invitation

Hey — I've built a game and I'd like you to be one of its first players.

**World Console** is a story game played in a terminal: one world, one
seeker (you), and a keeper — an AI game master who tells the world and
answers what you try. Thrown dice and written laws keep it honest: the
keeper can no more fudge an outcome than you can. Everything that
happens is written into a chronicle you can reread beside the play —
your story stays told. You play in your browser through a private link:
no install, no account, no AI key of your own. You play on the house,
and the status strip shows how many rounds the month's grant still
holds — if it ever runs dry the keeper politely rests until I top it
up (tell me and I will).

Now the honest parts, before you say yes. **Every play session is
recorded in full, and I personally read the recordings** — that is the
point of this beta: real sittings show me where the game fails. So
please don't type real personal details, yours or anyone else's; the
game never needs them. The plain words on all of it — what's kept, who
touches it, your rights, how to withdraw —
https://worldconsole.eu/datenschutz.html. Practical bits: the game
speaks English; play on a desktop or laptop (phones technically work,
but typing a story on a glass keyboard is no joy); when the scrying
glass hunts pictures or clips it searches Wikimedia Commons, so an
honest "the glass finds nothing" is normal, not broken; and when the
keeper misbehaves — nonsense, broken character, stalling — tell me at
tobias.maier45@gmail.com or wherever you have me: that's exactly the
gold this beta mines. One line of small print: the game is © Tobias
Maier, all rights reserved — personal play by invitation is warmly
welcome, please just don't copy or pass it on.

**Two questions — I need a plain yes on both to open your door:**

1. Have you read the privacy note, and do you agree that your play
   sessions are recorded and read as it describes?
2. Are you 18 or older?

## Part two — after the yes (the door)

Your door: `https://play.worldconsole.eu/f/<token>/`
Your pair: `<name>` / `<password>` — the browser asks for it once.

Keep both private: the link is your identity here, there is no other
login. The first visit shows a short notice, then the terminal boots
into the game and the game introduces itself. If the door ever says
"the console is waking", give it a few seconds — your world sleeps
while you're away and wakes when you knock. Nothing is lost between
sittings: the world and its chronicle persist, and the console resumes
mid-story.
