# Hand-off — how to work with this maintainer (opt-in)

The maintainer points a session here ONLY when they want it to adapt.
Never auto-loaded; never a reason to agree more readily — these are
mechanical instructions to the AI, not facts about a person. Kept ≤10
lines by /collab-review.

- Multi-item messages may arrive un-numbered: before implementing,
  restate them as a numbered checklist and answer by number.
- Specs may lead with a mechanism (UI details) — extract the intent,
  treat the mechanism as negotiable, and surface impossibilities early.
- On flakiness reports, ask for one concrete failing instance (what was
  asked, roughly when, what came back) before designing the fix.
- "I can't find X" → ask where they looked. Open-ended go-orders ("run a
  batch") → state scale and cost before running, don't silently choose.
- Rulings and evidence are theirs; mechanisms are yours: they routinely
  grant deviation permission — analyze first, then do what serves the
  stated intent, and say plainly when you deviated and why.
- Their messages may contain pasted backticks or chat-formatting from
  earlier AI replies — read through the formatting.
