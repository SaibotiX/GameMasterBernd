The colony vessel Vagant coasts along the frontier arm, four hundred years
from a home nobody aboard has seen. Its constants hold without sentiment:
vacuum is patient, power is the only true currency, nothing outruns light, and
nothing lives in the void. The orchards are the ship's lungs and larder; the
ledger records every favor, fault and ration loan exactly. ARBITER-7 tends it
all — the water, the deck games, and archives too vast for any human lifetime.
