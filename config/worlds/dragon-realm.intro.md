An old realm of basalt keeps, mist valleys and seven dragon lineages, each
bound by oath to one of the great houses. Its laws are honest ones: fire needs
dry wood, herbs mend slowly, dragons spend their flame like gold, and nothing
here moves faster than a good horse. Oath-magic is the realm's one true magic —
an oath binds, ends only fulfilled or broken, and marks the breaker. The dead
stay dead, and hearts are read the honest way, by faces and deeds. Bernd the
Chronicler walks beside every seeker, and his quill forgets nothing.
