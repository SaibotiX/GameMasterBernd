2. **Triggers.** Every "Revisit when" clause EVALUATED against

   actually fired? Fired → a flagged item with its evidence.
3. **⚠ facts.** Every fast-moving marked fact re-verified at its

   never dumps). Verdicts go in the sweep log, not the entries.
4. **Time backstop.** Entries untouched ~3 months with no fired
   trigger: FLAG only — the question is "did a trigger fire
   unnoticed?", never re-litigation for its own sake.
5. **Contradictions.** Entries against entries; entries against the
   documents that point at them; command files against how the work
   actually runs now — two homes must never answer one question
   differently.
6. **Icebox pass** (R25): each On-ice entry — condition fired
   (propose revival), still live (stays), or twice-untouched (propose
   Closed or re-argue).
7. **Report.** Per finding: a dated amendment / supersession /
   tombstone DRAFT with its evidence, for the maintainer's ruling —
   adopt, adapt, decline. The sweep lands as a dated research-log
   entry (06) recording scope, findings AND the still-valid verdicts,
   so confirmation is recorded, never silent.
